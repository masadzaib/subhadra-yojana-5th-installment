Subhadra Yojana 5th Installment: August 2026 Expected Date, Amount & Steps

📅 June 2026 📂 Subhadra Yojana ⏱️ 4 min read

Subhadra Yojana ki 5th installment of ₹5,000 August 2026 mein expected hai. Odisha government ka Raksha Bandhan ke occasion per payment release karne ka pattern raha hai, aur is baar 28 August 2026 (Friday) ko Raksha Bandhan hai. Official date abhi WCD department ne announce nahi ki hai.

Pichli (4th) installment 8 March 2026 ko International Women's Day per over 1 crore women ke accounts mein credit hui thi. Is pattern ke hisaab se August 2026 naturally next slot hai. Social media per jo specific dates circulate ho rahi hain wo abhi unconfirmed hain.

Har eligible woman ko ₹10,000 per year milte hain, do installments mein: ek August (Raksha Bandhan) aur ek March (Women's Day). Yeh 6-month cycle hi August 2026 ko 5th installment ka expected slot banata hai.

✅ 5th Installment se Pehle Yeh Steps Complete Karein

1
Face Authentication e-KYC Complete Karein

2026 installments ke liye face-based e-KYC mandatory hai. Agar pending hai to payment hold ho sakti hai. Subhadra portal ya nearest support centre per complete karein. Status "e-KYC Complete" aana chahiye.

2
Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI Mapping Fix Karein

DBT payment sirf Aadhaar-seeded aur NPCI-mapped account mein aati hai. Yeh sabse common reason hai transfer bounce hone ka. Bank jayen, "Aadhaar seeding / NPCI mapping" request karein.

3
Account Active aur Single-Holder Rakhen

Account single-holder, active, aur DBT-enabled hona chahiye. Agar joint account hai to DBT transfer fail ho sakta hai.

4
Beneficiary Status Check Karein

Official portal per apna status check karein. "Approved" status matlab aap next transfer ke liye clear hain. Agar 2 weeks se zyada pending ho to helpline 14678 per contact karein.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

5th installment ki official date announce hui hai?
Nahi. Abhi tak WCD department ne official date release nahi ki. Raksha Bandhan (28 August 2026) ke aas-paas expected hai, lekin confirm hone par hi portal per update aayega.
Kya 5th installment ka amount badh sakta hai?
Abhi tak koi verified announcement nahi aayi kisi increase ki. Amount ₹5,000 hi rahega.
Kya 5th installment ke liye dobara apply karna padega?
Nahi. Agar aap already verified beneficiary hain, payment automatically credit hoti hai. Sirf e-KYC aur bank seeding current rakhna zaroori hai.
4th installment nahi aayi to kya karein?
Portal per status check karein, e-KYC complete karein, NPCI mapping confirm karein, aur phir helpline 14678 per apne reference number ke saath contact karein.
